Commuter

Train schedules, ready for the week.

Commuter checks published train timetables each Monday, prepares weekday travel options, updates a dedicated Google Calendar, and emails a digest each week with changed times or confirmation that the schedule matches the previous week.

How it works

  • Reads public timetable data and filters the owner's selected departure windows.
  • Creates or updates schedule events and invitations in the owner's chosen calendar.
  • Sends weekly emails through the owner's Gmail account to the configured recipient, explaining changes or confirming an unchanged schedule.

This is a personal tool operated by itsazndy. It is not open for public registration. It does not book tickets or provide live train tracking.

Your Google account

Commuter requests permission to send email and manage events on calendars you own. Its Gmail permission does not allow reading your inbox. Access is used to deliver the schedule service you configure.
